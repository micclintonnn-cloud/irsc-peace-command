Who are
we?

The Inter-Religious Solidarity Council (IRSC) is a collective platform comprising a network of citizens committed to interfaith harmony, including faith leaders, activists, scholars, journalists and other conscientious voices.

IRSC seeks to provide a space for interfaith engagement grounded in the principles of mutual respect and dialogue.

IRSC Newsletter - November 2026

Our Vision

A harmonious India and world where people of all faiths and beliefs are free to practice their own faiths without encroaching on others, live in mutual respect, understanding and shared commitment to peace, justice and human dignity.

Our Mission

To engage in meaningful dialogue between diverse faith communities and followers of spiritual traditions to promote mutual understanding.

To protect cultural and religious diversity as a fundamental pillar of our shared heritage.

To take a stand against extremism, absolutism, and supremacist ideas promoted in the name of religions, faiths, and beliefs.

To promote spirituality and values of love, empathy, compassion, solidarity, and inclusiveness while encouraging simple living.

To collaborate with institutions to undertake socially relevant services that serve the needy and unite us all in solidarity.

To work towards building bridges across faith communities by addressing prejudicial attitudes and harmful stereotypes.

To promote peace, social justice, and harmonious relations between all faith communities in Mumbai.
